BountyVault

How it works

  1. Create. The sponsor locks the full bounty in the component vault. State: Open.
  2. Submit. A hunter submits a report_hash (SHA-256 of the report). The report itself never goes on chain. State: Submitted.
  3. Approve pays the whole vault to the hunter. Reject clears the submission and reopens the bounty. State: Paid / Open.
  4. Dispute. If the hunter disagrees with a rejection path, they dispute. Funds stay locked. State: Disputed.
  5. Resolve. The referee — fixed at creation, never changeable — pays the hunter or refunds the sponsor. State: Paid / Refunded.
  6. Cancel / timeout. The sponsor can cancel while Open; anyone can call timeout once the deadline epoch passes. Both refund the whole vault to the sponsor. State: Refunded.

Only hashes go on chain. The scope text and the report stay off-chain — that is the point: a public bounty board that does not leak what was found, or where.
